## Tous les Jours: https://tljus.com/stores/indianapolis-8448-center-run-dr?utm_source=google&utm_medium=gbp&utm_campaign=castleton

HOME PAGE:

- Image carousel on Home page with images of cakes with a clean off-white/cream background.
- Cakes are placed on cake stands
- Under img carousel the name of business, location, contact info, social media tags, About Hero section on bottom right
- Btns to submit online orders, View menu and get directions to the bakery
- Footer: social media tags, hyperlinks to pages
- Alert box appears to place orders for delivery or pickup - informs ability to track/manage online orders
- Right side collapsed navigation

  MENU:
  -Offers bakery items and beverages - simple close-up pictures
  Bakery Items: clickable img(s) to all bakery items: BREAD, CAKE, SANDWICHES, PASTRY, COFFEE, TEA & BLENDED.

## Pots & Pans Pie Co. : https://potsandpansindy.com/

Home Page:

- Centered navbar with links to frames of Menu, Contact ,FAQ, Online orders, Visiting locations and More tab that shows About, Catering Classes and Catering.
- Right corner clickable imgs for customer account, shopping cart and search button
- Hero Section with CTA shop now button
- Mission statement
- Customer review carousel
- CTA buttons for delivery and pick-up orders and tracking shipping
- Footer: displays social media tags: Facebook, IG and Twitter/X. Gives the address, newsletter subscription signup, Refund and Cancellation Policy hyperlink for viewing.
