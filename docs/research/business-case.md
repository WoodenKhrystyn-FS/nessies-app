- A/B Testing:
  - A practice that optimizes user experience and improves web and mobile performance = meets the users’ needs and improves business results
  - Split testing = compares 2 or more variables for digital assets (webpage, app or email) to find which gets better outcomes, higher engagement, conversions and click-through rates, basically who stays longer on the web interface.
  - Performance metrics aer analyzed and compared
  - Common uses of A/B Testing: Web design, email campaigns, product features and ads
  - Benefits: Data-driven in decisions.
  - Little risk of putting ineffective changes
  - User experience and metrics continue to improve.
  - Why do A/B Testing? optimizes user experience and improves web and mobile performance & higher engagement, conversions and click-through rates, Better ROI.
