# Week 1 Research - Project Clarification & Kickoff

## Project

Three Little Ladies Bakery Website

## Research Summary

This week I reviewed the provided course resources related to project proposals, discovery processes, project kickoff activities, and evaluating business ideas. The purpose of this research was to clarify the direction of my project and ensure it has a clear scope and business value before continuing development.

---

## Resource 1: Project Proposals and Getting Started

### Key Takeaways

A project proposal serves as a foundation for development by defining:

- Project goals
- Scope of work
- Deliverables
- Requirements
- Success criteria

### Application to My Project

After reviewing my original proposal, I confirmed that the primary goal of the Three Little Ladies Bakery Website is to provide a professional online presence for a local bakery while demonstrating my front-end development and UI/UX skills.

The project scope includes:

- Responsive website design
- Product showcase pages
- Business information
- Contact information
- User-friendly navigation

---

## Resource 2: Discovery Process

### Key Takeaways

The discovery phase helps identify user needs, business goals, and project requirements before development begins.

Important questions include:

- Who are the users?
- What problem is being solved?
- What value does the project provide?
- What features are necessary?

### Application to My Project

The target audience consists of local bakery customers who need quick access to product information and business details.

The project solves the problem of limited online visibility by providing customers with an easy-to-use website that showcases the bakery's products and services.

---

## Resource 3: Evaluating Startup Ideas

### Key Takeaways

A successful project should solve a real problem and provide value to users.

Projects should be evaluated based on:

- Customer need
- Business value
- Feasibility
- Long-term growth potential

### Application to My Project

The bakery website addresses a real business need by creating a stronger online presence and improving customer engagement. The project is feasible using my current skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and UI/UX design.

Future growth opportunities could include:

- Online ordering
- Customer reviews
- Event and catering inquiries
- Product availability updates

---

## Business Case

### Problem

The bakery has limited online visibility and lacks a modern website that effectively showcases products and services.

### Solution

Develop a responsive and user-friendly website that provides customers with information about the bakery while supporting future business growth.

### Benefits

- Increased visibility
- Improved customer engagement
- Better brand representation
- Enhanced user experience
- Portfolio-ready project demonstrating professional skills

---

## 30-Second Pitch

Three Little Ladies Bakery Website is a responsive web application designed to help a local bakery establish a professional online presence. The site allows customers to browse products, learn about the business, and easily find contact information. By focusing on modern UI/UX principles and responsive design, the project creates a positive user experience while helping the business reach more customers.
