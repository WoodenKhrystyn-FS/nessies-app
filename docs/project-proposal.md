### Three Little Ladies Bakery Website

## Updated Project Proposal

## Project Overview

Three Little Ladies Bakery is a responsive web application designed to provide a professional online presence for a local bakery. The website allows customers to browse bakery products, learn about the business and easily access contact information. The project serves as both a practical business solution and a portfolio piece displaying modern front-end development and UI/UX design skills.

## Problem Statement

Many small businesses rely primarly on social media for customer engagement and lack a dedicated website that effectively displays their products and services. Withut a centralized online presence, customers may hav a harder time finding information, veiwing avaiable products or contacting the business altogether.

## Proposed Solution

Develop a modern, responsive bakery website providiing:

    - Product showcase and gallery
    - Bakery information and branding
    - Contact and inquiry functionality
    - Responsive mobile-friendly design
    - Easy navigation and user-friendly experience

This websitew ill create a stronger online presence while improving accessibility for current and potential customers.
